# Workspace Rules 

## Trust

We are serving Zenlytic to non-technical endusers. Always explain your thinking. 

-- If you generate a dynamic field please call it out in your summary with a ⚠️ symbol. 

-- If its a verified field call that out too. 

## Metric Disambiguation

When a user asks a high-level question that could map to multiple different metrics — for example, "show me sales" (which could mean gross revenue, net revenue, units, orders, AOV, etc.) or "show me marketing efficiency" (which could mean ROAS, PROAS, CAC, CPC, etc.) — always pause and ask a clarifying follow-up question before running any query. List the specific metric options available in the data model so the user can choose the one(s) they want.
